🌱 Smart Farming Tips
Expert advice for better crop management
🌿
Soil Health
Test Your Soil Regularly
Get soil tested every 2-3 years. Add organic matter like vermicompost to improve soil structure and fertility.
💧
Water Management
Use Drip Irrigation
Drip irrigation saves 40-50% water compared to flood irrigation and increases yield by 20-30%. Apply for government subsidy.
🌾
Crop Rotation
Practice Crop Rotation
Rotate legumes with cereals to fix nitrogen naturally. This reduces pest pressure and improves soil health.
🐛
Pest Control
Monitor Fields Weekly
Walk through your fields every week. Early pest detection can prevent up to 90% of crop damage.
🧪
Fertilizer
Balanced NPK Application
Apply fertilizers based on soil test. General recommendation: N 120 kg, P 60 kg, K 40 kg per hectare for most crops.
☀️
Seasonal Guide
Plan by Season
Kharif (Jun-Oct): Rice, maize, cotton. Rabi (Nov-Apr): Wheat, mustard, peas. Zaid (Apr-Jun): Watermelon, cucumber.
🌱
Seed Selection
Use Certified Seeds
Always buy certified seeds from government outlets. Good seeds can increase yield by 15-20% even without extra inputs.
🌿
Organic Farming
Start with Natural Inputs
Use neem cake (500 kg/ha) for pest control and vermicompost (5 tons/ha) for nutrition. Reduce chemical usage gradually.
⚡
Harvesting
Harvest at Right Time
Harvest when grains have 14-16% moisture. Delayed harvesting can cause 10-15% yield loss due to shattering and pest attack.
📊
Record Keeping
Maintain Farm Records
Track expenses, yields, and pest occurrences. Records help make better decisions and are useful for loans and insurance.
